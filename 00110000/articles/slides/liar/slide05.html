Provenance

Every contradiction has an address and a chain of custody

Derivations are proof terms

basep(s,t,a,b) | reflp(x) | trans(π,ρ) | cpap,q,u(π,ρ)

The functor He sends a derivation to its history: every kernel entry used, every adoption event, and the perspective adopted from.

Provenance Theorem. Every derivation of a contradiction at e contains an identity entry between the roles, in the kernel of e or inside an adopted subterm, and the history records it with every perspective it passed through.

Reading a history

⟨base, e, Tgt, Ptr, x, ¬x⟩
⟨adopt, e, q, Tgt⟩
⟨base, q, Ptr, Tgt, ¬x, ¬x⟩

The second line says that e took a judgment from q; the third names the identity entry that q supplied. The contradiction at e was inherited, and the record says from whom.

A hierarchy forbids the identification; this account permits it, records it, and exhibits it as the sole cause
